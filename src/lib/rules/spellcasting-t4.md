# Tier 4 Spells

## Tier 4 Priest Spells

**Banish**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One extraplanar creature must make a WIS check vs your spellcasting roll or be banished to its home plane. If you maintain focus for the full duration, the banishment is permanent.

**Heal**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- One creature you touch regains 4d8+4 HP.

**Raise Dead**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- A creature that died within the last 3 days is restored to life with 1 HP. The creature takes a permanent -1 penalty to all rolls until a full rest.

**Restore**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- Remove all stat damage, blindness, deafness, or paralysis from one creature you touch.

## Tier 4 Wizard Spells

**Dimension Door**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Far
- You teleport to a location you can see or describe within far range. You can bring one willing creature of your size or smaller.

**Polymorph**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- Transform one creature into a beast of equal or lower level. The target uses the beast's stats but retains its own mind. At 0 HP, it reverts.

**Stoneskin**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One creature you touch resists all non-magical physical damage (half damage).

**Wall of Fire**
- Tier: 4 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- A wall of fire up to near distance long and 20 ft high appears. Creatures that pass through take 4d6 fire damage. Creatures within close range of one side (your choice) take 2d6 fire damage at the start of their turn.
