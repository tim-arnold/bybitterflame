# Combat

## Initiative

1. Each player rolls **d20 + DEX modifier**.
2. GM rolls once for all enemies as a group.
3. Highest result goes first.
4. Play proceeds **clockwise** from the first actor.
5. Ties: players go before monsters. Between players, higher DEX goes first.

## Turn Structure

On your turn you get **one action** and **one move**.

### Movement

- Move up to **near** distance (30 ft) on your turn.
- Movement can be split (move, act, move remainder).
- **Forgo your action** to move near a second time (double move = 60 ft total).

### Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Melee Attack | d20 + STR mod + bonuses vs target AC. Must be at close range (5 ft). |
| Ranged Attack | d20 + DEX mod + bonuses vs target AC. Range varies by weapon (close/near/far). |
| Cast a Spell | Use your action to cast one spell (see spellcasting rules). |
| Use an Item | Draw a potion, activate an object, etc. |
| Improvise | Any creative action: shove, grapple, disarm, trip, throw oil, etc. GM sets DC. |
| Multitask | Combine two simple actions (e.g., draw weapon + open door). Neither can be an attack or spell. |
| Dash | Forgo action to move near a second time. |

### Free Actions

- Speaking a few words.
- Dropping an item.
- Dropping prone.

## Attacks

### Melee Attack
**d20 + STR modifier + bonuses >= target AC = hit.**

- Finesse weapons may use DEX instead of STR.

### Ranged Attack
**d20 + DEX modifier + bonuses >= target AC = hit.**

- Firing into melee: the attacker has **disadvantage**.

### Critical Hit (Natural 20)
- Automatic hit.
- Roll **double the weapon's damage dice**, then add modifiers.

### Critical Miss (Natural 1)
- Automatic miss regardless of bonuses.

## Damage

- On a hit, roll **weapon damage die + relevant ability modifier + bonuses**.
- Minimum damage on a hit is always 1.
- Damage reduces the target's HP.

## Dying and Death

1. At **0 HP**, a character is **dying** and falls unconscious.
2. Roll a **death timer: 1d4 + CON modifier** rounds (minimum 1 round).
3. When the timer reaches 0, the character **dies**.
4. Each round while dying, the character rolls **d20**:
   - **Natural 20:** Stabilize and regain 1 HP. You are conscious but prone.
5. **Stabilize by ally:** Another creature at close range makes a **DC 15 INT check**. On success, the dying creature stabilizes at 0 HP (unconscious but no longer dying).
6. **Healing:** Any magical healing on a dying creature restores HP normally and ends the dying state.
7. **Massive damage:** If a single hit reduces you to negative HP equal to your max HP, you die instantly.

## Morale

- When enemies are reduced to **half their number** or the leader drops to **half HP**, the GM makes a **DC 15 WIS check** for the enemy group.
- **Failure:** The enemies flee, surrender, or retreat.
- **Success:** They continue fighting.
- Mindless creatures (undead, constructs) and fanatics are immune to morale.

## Cover and Terrain

| Cover Type | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Half cover (low wall, tree) | Attackers have **disadvantage** on attack rolls against the target |
| Full cover (solid wall, pillar) | Target **cannot be targeted** by attacks or most spells |
| Difficult terrain (rubble, water, dense brush) | Movement costs **double** (near = 15 ft effective) |

## Surprise

- Creatures that are **undetected** when combat starts get a **surprise round**.
- In the surprise round, only the surprising side acts.
- After the surprise round, roll initiative normally.
- Goblins with Keen Senses cannot be surprised.

## Special Combat Situations

### Two-Weapon Fighting
- Not a standard rule. Attacks use one weapon per turn.

### Grappling
- Melee attack roll vs target's AC. On hit, target is grappled (cannot move). Target can break free with a STR check vs grappler's STR check on their turn.

### Shoving
- STR check vs target's STR check. On success, push target 5 ft or knock prone.

### Prone
- Melee attacks against prone targets have advantage.
- Ranged attacks against prone targets have disadvantage.
- Standing from prone costs half your movement.
