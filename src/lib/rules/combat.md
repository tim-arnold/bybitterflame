# Combat

## Initiative

When combat begins:

1. Each player rolls **d20 + DEX modifier**. The GM makes a single roll for all enemies.
2. Turns proceed in **descending order** — highest roll acts first, then the next, and so on.
3. On a tie, players act before monsters. If two players tie, they choose who goes first.

Initiative order stays fixed for the entire fight. Do not re-roll each round.

## Turn Structure

Each turn, a character gets **one action** and **one move** in any order. Movement can be split around the action (move partway, act, finish moving).

### Movement

A character can move up to **near** distance (30 ft) on their turn. To cover more ground, a character may **sacrifice their action** and move near a second time — 60 ft total that turn.

### Actions

**Attack (Melee):** Roll d20 + STR mod + bonuses against the target's AC. Must be at close range (5 ft). Precise weapons may use DEX instead.

**Attack (Ranged):** Roll d20 + DEX mod + bonuses against the target's AC. Weapon range determines maximum distance (close/near/far). Firing at a target engaged in melee imposes **disadvantage**.

**Cast a Spell:** Cast one spell. Pay the Toll cost and resolve effects per the spellcasting rules.

**Use an Item:** Draw and drink a potion, activate a magic object, light a torch, etc.

**Improvise:** Anything creative — shove, grapple, disarm, trip, throw oil, kick a table. The GM sets a DC or calls for an opposed check.

**Dash:** Give up your action to move near a second time (same as sacrificing your action for extra movement).

Two trivial actions that don't require an attack or spell (drawing a weapon while opening a door, for instance) can be combined into a single turn at the GM's discretion.

### Free Actions

These cost nothing and can be done on any turn:
- Speaking a short sentence
- Dropping a held item
- Falling prone

## Attacks and Damage

### Hitting

**d20 + ability modifier + bonuses >= target AC = hit.** STR governs melee; DEX governs ranged. Precise weapons use DEX for both.

### Damage

On a hit, roll the **weapon's damage die + the relevant ability modifier + any bonuses**. Damage is subtracted from the target's HP. A hit always deals at least 1 damage, even if modifiers reduce the roll below zero.

### Critical Hit (Natural 20)

An automatic hit. Roll the weapon's damage dice **twice** and sum the results, then add modifiers once.

### Critical Miss (Natural 1)

An automatic miss, regardless of modifiers or bonuses.

## Dying and Death

1. At **0 HP**, a character falls unconscious and is **dying**.
2. They have at most **3 rounds** before death.
3. Each round while dying, roll a **d6 — the Fate Roll**:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| **1-2** | **Dead.** The character dies immediately. |
| **3-5** | **Still dying.** The countdown continues. |
| **6** | **Stabilize.** The character stabilizes at 0 HP (unconscious, no longer dying). **Only if CON is 14+.** If CON is 13 or below, treat as still dying. |

4. If **3 rounds pass** without the character dying, stabilizing, or being saved — **they die.** No extensions.
5. **Stabilize by ally:** A creature at close range makes a **DC 16 WIS check**. On success, the dying character stabilizes at 0 HP (unconscious but no longer dying).
6. **Healing:** Any magical healing on a dying character restores HP normally and ends the dying state.

### Advantage on Fate Rolls

Some abilities grant advantage on death fate rolls (Revenant ancestry, Marauder talents). Roll **2d6 and take the best result.** This shifts the lethal range — a single 1 kills you instead of 1-2. The 3-round cap still applies.

## Morale

- When enemies are reduced to **half their number** or their leader drops to **half HP**, the GM makes a **DC 13 CON check** for the enemy group.
- **Failure:** The enemies flee, surrender, or retreat.
- **Success:** They continue fighting.
- Mindless creatures and fanatics are immune to morale.

## Cover and Terrain

**Partial cover** (crouching behind a low wall, standing behind a tree trunk): Attacks against the target have **disadvantage**.

**Total cover** (completely behind a solid wall or pillar): The target **cannot be attacked** directly. Area effects may still reach them at the GM's discretion.

**Rough ground** (rubble, knee-deep water, thick undergrowth): Movement through the area costs **double** — moving near distance covers only 15 ft.

## Surprise

- Creatures that are **undetected** when combat starts get a **surprise round**.
- In the surprise round, only the surprising side acts.
- After the surprise round, roll initiative normally.

## Special Combat Situations

### Two-Weapon Fighting
- Not a standard rule. Attacks use one weapon per turn.

### Grappling
- Melee attack roll vs target's AC. On hit, target is grappled (cannot move). Target can break free with a STR check vs grappler's STR check on their turn.

### Shoving
- STR check vs target's STR check. On success, push target 5 ft or knock prone.

### Prone
- Melee attacks against prone targets have advantage.
- Ranged attacks against prone targets have disadvantage.
- Standing from prone costs half your movement.
