# Tier 1 Spells

## Tier 1 Priest Spells

**Cure Wounds**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- Heal one creature you touch for 1d8 HP.

**Holy Weapon**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One weapon you touch deals +1d6 radiant damage on each hit.

**Light**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: 1 hour (real time) | Range: Close
- An object you touch sheds light in a near radius. Can be cast on a held item.

**Protection from Evil**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One creature you touch gains +1 AC against attacks from chaotic creatures and advantage on saves against their spells and abilities.

**Shield of Faith**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One creature you touch gains +2 AC.

**Turn Undead**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Priest | Duration: 1d4+1 rounds | Range: Near
- Make a spellcasting check (DC 10 + highest undead level). On success, 1d4 + priest level nearest undead within near range flee.

## Tier 1 Wizard Spells

**Burning Hands**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Close (cone)
- A cone of fire bursts from your hands. All creatures in a close-range cone take 1d6 fire damage.

**Charm Person**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One humanoid you can see must make a WIS check vs your spellcasting roll or regard you as a trusted friend. Broken if you or your allies harm it.

**Detect Magic**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- You sense the presence and location of magic within near range. You can determine the school of magic with a DC 12 INT check.

**Feather Fall**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Near
- Up to 5 creatures within near range fall gently, taking no falling damage. Lasts until they land.

**Floating Disk**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- A floating disk of force appears that can carry up to 20 gear slots of weight. It hovers 3 feet off the ground and follows you.

**Light**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: 1 hour (real time) | Range: Close
- An object you touch sheds light in a near radius.

**Magic Missile**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Far
- A bolt of force strikes one creature you can see for 1d4+1 damage. Automatically hits (no attack roll), but still requires a spellcasting check.

**Sleep**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: 1d4 rounds | Range: Near
- 2d8 HP worth of creatures within near range fall asleep, starting with the lowest HP creature. Undead and creatures immune to charm are unaffected.

**Shield**
- Tier: 1 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Self
- You gain +2 AC. While focusing on this spell, you cannot cast other focus spells.
