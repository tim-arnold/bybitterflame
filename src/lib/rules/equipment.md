# Equipment

## Currency

| Currency | Abbr | Value |
|----------|------|-------|
| Gold piece | gp | 1 gp |
| Silver piece | sp | 1/10 gp |
| Copper piece | cp | 1/100 gp |

**10 sp = 1 gp. 100 cp = 1 gp. 10 cp = 1 sp.**

Coins: 100 coins = 1 gear slot.

## Gear Slots

- A character can carry a number of items equal to their **STR score or 10**, whichever is higher.
- Most items take **1 gear slot**.
- Some heavy items take **2 or 3 slots** (noted in tables).
- Small items (coins, gems, arrows) can be bundled: 100 coins = 1 slot, 20 arrows = 1 slot.
- Worn armor occupies gear slots as noted.

### Weapon Properties

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Finesse** | Use STR or DEX for attack and damage rolls. |
| **Loading** | Requires an action to reload after each shot. You can fire and reload in the same turn but cannot attack again. |
| **Thrown** | Can be used as a ranged weapon at near range. Uses STR for attack/damage. |
| **Two-handed** | Requires both hands to wield. Cannot use a shield. |
| **Versatile** | Can be used one-handed or two-handed. Two-handed damage die is shown in parentheses. |

### Mithral Armor

- Mithral versions of metal armor (chainmail, plate) cost 10x normal price.
- **Mithral chainmail:** 1 slot (instead of 2), no stealth/swim penalty. 600 gp.
- **Mithral plate:** 2 slots (instead of 3), no stealth/swim penalty. 1,300 gp.

## Crawling Kit

Every character begins with a **crawling kit** in addition to purchased gear. It contains:

- Backpack
- Flint and steel
- Torch (x2)
- Rations (3 days)
- Rope (60 ft)
- 2 iron spikes

The crawling kit takes **0 additional slots** beyond its individual items (backpack is worn, small items bundled). Total: approximately 4 gear slots.
