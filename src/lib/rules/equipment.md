# Equipment

## Currency

| Currency | Abbr | Value |
|----------|------|-------|
| Gold piece | gp | 1 gp |
| Silver piece | sp | 1/10 gp |
| Copper piece | cp | 1/100 gp |

**10 sp = 1 gp. 100 cp = 1 gp. 10 cp = 1 sp.**

Coins: first 100 coins are free to carry; every 100 coins after that takes 1 gear slot.

## Gear Slots

- A character can carry a number of items equal to their **STR score** (minimum 8).
- Most items take **1 gear slot**.
- Some heavy items take **2 or 3 slots** (noted in tables).
- Small items (coins, gems, arrows) can be bundled: 100 coins = 1 slot (first 100 free), 20 arrows = 1 slot.
- Worn armor occupies gear slots as noted.

### Weapon Properties

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Precise** | Use DEX instead of STR for attack and damage rolls. |
| **Slow** | After firing, you must use your move to reload before firing again. |
| **Thrown** | Can be hurled at near range. Uses STR for the attack and damage rolls. |
| **Two-handed** | Requires both hands. Cannot carry a shield while wielding this weapon. |
| **Versatile** | Wield one-handed or two-handed. Damage listed as one-hand/two-hand. |

### Knocker-Forged Armor

Knocker smiths work metals using techniques preserved from the old Underways. Their armor is lighter, quieter, and extraordinarily rare.

- Knocker-forged versions of metal armor cost **8× the base price**.
- **Knocker-forged mail:** 1 slot (instead of 2), no stealth/swim penalty. 600 gp.
- **Knocker-forged plate:** 2 slots (instead of 3), no swim restriction, no stealth penalty. 1,200 gp.

## Delver's Kit

A delver's kit costs **5 gp** and uses **5 gear slots**. It contains:

- Backpack (0 slots, worn)
- Flint and steel (0 slots)
- Torches ×2 (2 slots)
- Rations, 3 days (1 slot)
- Rope, 60 ft (1 slot)
- Chalk, 3 pieces (0 slots)
- Waterskin (1 slot)
