# Running Adventures

## Dungeon Crawling

1. Describe the room: exits, obvious features, lighting, sounds, smells.
2. Ask: "What do you do?"
3. Resolve actions. Call for checks only when needed.
4. Track time (delve turns). At the appropriate interval for the area's danger level, roll 1d6 — on a 1, generate an encounter using the encounter design rules and apply a reaction roll if disposition is unknown.
5. Track light. Announce when torches are getting low.

## Overland Travel

1. Describe the terrain and weather.
2. Roll for random encounters (1 on d6 per hex or per 5 hours).
3. Track rations consumed.
4. Describe landmarks and points of interest.

## Town and Social

1. Describe the location and notable NPCs.
2. Let players drive the conversation.
3. Use reaction rolls for new NPC encounters.
4. Provide rumors, quest hooks, and shopping opportunities.
5. Offer downtime activities if the characters have returned from an adventure and have gold to spend (Carousing for Fighters, Wheeling & Dealing for Rogues, Studying for Casters).
