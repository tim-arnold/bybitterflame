# Tier 5 Spells

## Tier 5 Priest Spells

**Divine Intervention**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Self
- Your deity directly intervenes. Describe your plea. The GM determines the outcome, which should be miraculous but not omnipotent.

**Flame Strike**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Far
- A column of divine fire strikes a near-radius area. All creatures in the area take 6d8 radiant/fire damage (half on successful DEX check vs your spellcasting roll).

**True Resurrection**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- A creature that has been dead for any length of time is restored to life with full HP, provided its soul is willing. Requires 1,000 gp worth of sacred materials consumed in the casting.

## Tier 5 Wizard Spells

**Cloudkill**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Far
- A near-radius cloud of toxic gas fills an area. Creatures inside take 4d6 poison damage at the start of each turn (half on CON check vs your spellcasting roll). The cloud moves near distance away from you each round.

**Hold Monster**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One creature must make a WIS check vs your spellcasting roll or be paralyzed. It repeats the check at the end of each of its turns.

**Teleport**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- You and up to 5 willing creatures you touch teleport to a location you are familiar with, on the same plane of existence.

**Power Word Kill**
- Tier: 5 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Near
- One creature you can see with 50 HP or fewer dies instantly. No saving throw.
