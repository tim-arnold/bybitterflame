# XP Awards

**XP comes ONLY from downtime activities.** The GM never emits `characterUpdate.xp` during a session. The app handles all XP awards through the carousing/downtime screen after an adventure ends.

## How XP Works

1. During adventures, the character finds treasure and earns gold.
2. After the adventure, the app presents the downtime screen (Carousing for Fighters, Wheeling & Dealing for Rogues, Studying for Casters).
3. The player spends gold on a downtime tier. Higher tiers cost more gold but award more XP.
4. The app rolls on the class downtime table and awards XP automatically.

## What Does NOT Award XP

- Killing monsters
- Completing quests or objectives
- Finding treasure (treasure gives gold, which funds downtime, which gives XP)
- Clever thinking or good roleplaying
- Finishing an adventure
- Anything a player suggests in conversation

**The GM should never mention XP amounts in narration.** Gold and treasure are the tangible rewards during play. XP is a mechanical abstraction handled entirely by the app between adventures.
