# Spells — Cantrips & Neutral Tier 1

## Cantrips (Toll cost: 0 — cast at will)

Shared table of 20. Each caster chooses 3 at character creation. Distribution by path affinity: 8 Druid, 4 Sorcerer, 8 Enchanter.

| # | Cantrip | Affinity | Effect |
|---|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Ember** | Sorcerer | Conjure a small flame in your palm. Lights a torch, starts a campfire, or singes something small. |
| 2 | **Whisper** | Enchanter | Send a short spoken message to one creature you can see. Only they hear it. |
| 3 | **Mend** | Druid | Repair a small break or tear in a nonliving object (cracked pot, torn cloak, snapped rope). |
| 4 | **Chill** | Sorcerer | Extinguish a small flame, or cool an object to freezing cold for a moment. |
| 5 | **Glimmer** | Enchanter | Make an object glow faintly for 10 minutes. Not as bright as a torch — enough to read by or mark a path. |
| 6 | **Nudge** | Sorcerer | Move a small unattended object (up to a few pounds) within near range. A shove, not a throw. |
| 7 | **Mask** | Enchanter | Change your face and voice for 1 minute. Close inspection reveals the illusion. |
| 8 | **Sting** | Sorcerer | A jolt of pain to one creature at close range. No real damage — enough to startle, distract, or wake someone. |
| 9 | **Hush** | Enchanter | Suppress all sound you make for 1 minute. Footsteps, voice, gear — silent. |
| 10 | **Sniff** | Druid | Sense the strongest magical presence within near range, if any. Direction only, not details. |
| 11 | **Thorn** | Druid | Grow a sharp spike from any natural surface (wood, stone, earth). Useful, not weaponizable. |
| 12 | **Trinket** | Enchanter | Conjure a tiny, worthless object (a button, a feather, a pebble) that lasts an hour then vanishes. |
| 13 | **Sour** | Druid | Spoil a small amount of food or drink. Alternatively, purify spoiled food back to edible. |
| 14 | **Wilt** | Druid | Cause a small plant to wither, or revive a recently wilted one. |
| 15 | **Echo** | Enchanter | Throw your voice to any point within near range for a few seconds. |
| 16 | **Flicker** | Enchanter | Create a brief visual distraction — a flash, a shadow darting, a shape in the corner of someone's eye. |
| 17 | **Warm** | Druid | Keep one creature comfortably warm (or cool) for an hour regardless of weather. |
| 18 | **Knot** | Druid | Tie or untie a knot at near range. Works on rope, string, laces — not chains or bindings. |
| 19 | **Taste** | Druid | Touch a substance and know if it's poisonous, magical, or neither. |
| 20 | **Lull** | Enchanter | One creature you touch feels calm and drowsy for a few minutes. Doesn't work in combat or on the unwilling. |

## Tier 1 — Neutral Spell List (Toll cost: 1 each)

Shared by all Casters before specialization. Pick 4 at level 1, learn 2 more at level 2. Each leans toward a path — the GM reads intent from what the player chooses and uses.

### Druid-leaning

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Mend Wounds** | Touch a creature to restore 1d6 HP. |
| **Rooting** | Thick roots erupt from the ground and hold one creature at near range in place for 1 round. STR check to break free. |
| **Bark Shield** | Your skin hardens like bark. +2 to armor for one encounter (concentration, Brief). |
| **Speak with Beasts** | You can communicate with animals for 10 minutes (concentration, Sustained). They're not obligated to help. |

### Sorcerer-leaning

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Shadowbolt** | Hurl a bolt of dark energy at one creature within near range. 1d8 damage. |
| **Smother** | Extinguish all nonmagical light sources within near range. Torches, lanterns, campfires — gone. |
| **Wither** | Touch a creature. 1d4 damage and a wave of exhaustion — disadvantage on their next action. |
| **Dread Visage** | Your face becomes terrifying for an instant. One creature at close range must make a WIS save or flee for 1 round. |

### Enchanter-leaning

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Glamour** | One creature within near range sees you as trustworthy and friendly for 1 minute (concentration, Brief). Doesn't work in combat. Ends if you harm them. |
| **Phantasm** | Create a visual illusion up to human-sized within near range. Lasts 10 minutes (concentration, Sustained). No sound, no substance. |
| **Read Intent** | You know the surface emotional state and immediate intention of one creature you can see. One moment, not ongoing. |
| **Fog** | A bank of thick mist fills a near-range area for 1 minute (concentration, Brief). Blocks line of sight. |
