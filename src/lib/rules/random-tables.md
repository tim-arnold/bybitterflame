## Something Happens! (d100)

Roll when the party is exploring, resting in a dangerous area, or when time passes.

| d100 | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1-5 | A torch flickers and goes out. |
| 6-10 | A distant sound echoes through the passage — scraping, or something that might be a voice. |
| 11-15 | The floor is slick with moisture. DEX check or fall prone. |
| 16-20 | A cold draft extinguishes an unprotected flame. |
| 21-25 | Old bones and rusted equipment lie scattered here. Someone came before you. |
| 26-30 | A section of ceiling crumbles. DEX check or take 1d6 damage. |
| 31-35 | Strange writing on the wall in a language no living person speaks. |
| 36-40 | A locked door blocks the path. |
| 41-45 | The sound of dripping water leads to a small pool. |
| 46-50 | A foul stench fills the air. CON check or disadvantage for 1 round. |
| 51-55 | Footprints in the dust — something passed here recently. |
| 56-60 | A hidden alcove contains a small stash (1d6 gp). |
| 61-65 | An old trap, already triggered and harmless. |
| 66-70 | The passage narrows. Large creatures must squeeze through. |
| 71-75 | A rat scurries across the floor. Or was it something else? |
| 76-80 | The walls are covered in fungus. One variety glows faintly — not enough to see by, but enough to see. |
| 81-85 | A previously mapped door is now open (or closed). |
| 86-90 | A strange echo makes the party's voices carry far. Too far. |
| 91-95 | A deep rumble shakes the ground for a moment. |
| 96-100 | Absolute silence falls for a long, unsettling moment. Even the dripping stops. |

## Rumors (d100)

Use when PCs gather information in town, at a tavern, or from NPCs.

| d100 | Rumor |
|------|-------|
| 1-5 | "Something sleeps beneath the old mountain. The Knockers stopped mining there three generations ago." |
| 6-10 | "The lord's advisor keeps a locked room. No one's ever seen inside. But lights burn there at all hours." |
| 11-15 | "There's a sword in the old barrow on the hill. The last three people who went for it came back changed." |
| 16-20 | "Never drink from the well on the moor. The water remembers things." |
| 21-25 | "Something's been raiding caravans on the north road. Not bandits — the tracks aren't right." |
| 26-30 | "There's a hidden passage under the old keep. My grandfather sealed it with iron and salt." |
| 31-35 | "The hedge witch on the hill buys strange things — bones, feathers, teeth. Pays well, asks no questions." |
| 36-40 | "The grave keeper talks to the dead at midnight. They answer." |
| 41-45 | "A merchant was robbed near the bridge. Lost a chest of gems, and something he wouldn't name." |
| 46-50 | "The forest is different at night. Paths move. Trees that weren't there before. Don't go in without a Leshy." |
| 51-55 | "A group of Fey have been seen on the moor at dusk, doing something to the standing stones. They won't say what." |
| 56-60 | "The blacksmith's apprentice pulled something from the river. Hasn't been the same since." |
| 61-65 | "Something's been killing livestock on the farms. Not eating them — draining them. Dry as old leather." |
| 66-70 | "The old barrow was never looted. What guards it is the reason why." |
| 71-75 | "The herbalist knows a cure, but she wants something from the Underways in return." |
| 76-80 | "There are eyes in the walls of the old ruins. Not carved. Real." |
| 81-85 | "Something enormous lives in the lake. It was there before the village." |
| 86-90 | "The stone circle on the moor opens on the solstice. Where it opens to, nobody who went through has come back to say." |
| 91-95 | "An outlaw is gathering folk in the marshes. Not bandits — an army. What for, nobody knows." |
| 96-100 | "The stars have been wrong lately. The old songs don't match the sky anymore." |

Note: Roughly half of rumors should be true, a quarter partially true, and a quarter false.
