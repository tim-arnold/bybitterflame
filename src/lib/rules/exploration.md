# Exploration

## Crawling Rounds

Outside of combat, time is tracked in **crawling rounds**. A crawling round represents roughly 10 minutes of in-game time. During a crawling round, the party can:

- Move through a few rooms or down a corridor
- Search a room
- Pick a lock or disarm a trap
- Have a brief conversation with an NPC

## Distances

| Term | Distance | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Close | 5 ft | Melee range, touching |
| Near | 30 ft | A large room, torch light radius |
| Far | Within sight | Across a cavern, down a long hall |

## Light and Darkness

### Light Sources

| Source | Radius | Duration | Notes |
|--------|--------|----------|-------|
| Torch | Near (30 ft) | 1 hour real time | Occupies 1 hand or can be set down |
| Lantern | 2x Near (60 ft) | 1 hour per oil flask | Occupies 1 hand, requires oil |
| Light spell | Near (30 ft) | 1 hour real time | Cast on an object, no hands needed |
| Candle | Close (5 ft) | 1 hour real time | Dim, fragile |

### Darkness Rules

- **Total darkness:** Characters without a light source have **disadvantage on all rolls** that require sight.
- **Random encounters in darkness:** Check for a random encounter **every crawling round** (instead of normal frequency).
- Creatures with darkvision can see in darkness as if dim light to near range.

### Light Timer Management

- Track torch/lantern duration in **real time** at the table (or approximated by the GM).
- When a light source burns out, the party is in darkness unless another is lit.
- **New light source:** Lighting a new torch or refueling a lantern **restarts** the timer from the full duration.
- Multiple light sources do not stack duration -- they burn simultaneously.

## Movement

### Climbing

- Requires a **STR or DEX check** (GM sets DC based on surface).
- Climbing speed is **half normal** movement.
- Failure by **5 or more** on the check = the character **falls**.

### Falling

- **1d6 damage per 10 feet fallen.**
- Maximum 20d6 (200 ft).

### Swimming

- Movement speed is **half** while swimming.
- A character can **hold their breath** for a number of rounds equal to their **CON modifier** (minimum 1 round).
- After that, the character must make a **DC 12 CON check** each round or begin drowning (0 HP at start of next turn).
- **Chainmail:** Disadvantage on swimming checks.
- **Plate:** Cannot swim at all; sinks immediately.

## Stealth and Surprise

### Stealth

- A creature attempting to move undetected makes a **DEX check** opposed by the observer's **WIS check**.
- A group uses the **lowest** DEX check among them.
- Advantage on stealth from Thief's Thievery ability and similar features.

### Surprise

- If one side is unaware, the aware side gets a **surprise round** (one full turn each) before initiative is rolled.
- **Goblins** (Keen Senses) are never surprised.

### Detecting

- Actively searching for traps, hidden doors, or concealed creatures: **INT or WIS check** (GM sets DC).
- Passive awareness (hearing approaching enemies, noticing something off): **WIS check** at GM discretion.

## Resting

### Full Rest

- Requires **8 hours of sleep** and consuming **1 ration**.
- Restores **all HP** and **all stat damage**.
- Restores all **lost spells** (except deity-revoked priest spells).
- If the rest is **interrupted** (combat, alarm, etc.), the character must make a **DC 12 CON check** or gain no benefit from the rest.

### No Rations

- A character who cannot eat during a rest does not regain HP or stat damage.

## Overland Travel

| Mode | Time per Hex (6 miles) |
|------|----------------------|
| Walking | 4 hours |
| Mounted | 2 hours |
| Sailing | 1 hour |

- A party can travel up to **8 hours per day** before needing to rest.
- Forced march beyond 8 hours: **DC 12 CON check** each additional hour or gain 1 level of exhaustion (disadvantage on all rolls, cumulative).

### Terrain Modifiers

| Terrain | Travel Time Modifier |
|---------|---------------------|
| Road | Normal |
| Plains/grassland | Normal |
| Forest | x1.5 |
| Hills | x1.5 |
| Mountains | x2 |
| Swamp | x2 |
| Desert | x1.5 |

## Random Encounters

### Encounter Frequency

| Area Danger | Check Frequency | Trigger |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| Unsafe | Every 3 crawling rounds | 1 on d6 |
| Risky | Every 2 crawling rounds | 1 on d6 |
| Deadly | Every crawling round | 1 on d6 |

- Roll **1d6**. On a result of **1**, an encounter occurs.
- In total darkness, check **every round** regardless of area danger.

## Reaction Check

When encountering creatures whose disposition is unknown, roll **2d6 + CHA modifier** of the speaking character.

| Roll | Reaction |
|------|----------|
| 0-6 | **Hostile.** Attacks, threatens, or acts aggressively. |
| 7-8 | **Suspicious.** Distrustful, demands answers, may attack if provoked. |
| 9 | **Neutral.** Indifferent, willing to talk but not helpful. |
| 10-11 | **Curious.** Interested, open to negotiation, mildly friendly. |
| 12+ | **Friendly.** Helpful, willing to aid, share information, or trade. |

## Doors

- **Stuck doors:** STR check to force open (DC 12 typical).
- **Locked doors:** Thief can pick (DEX check, Thievery advantage). Otherwise, force open at higher DC or find the key.
- **Secret doors:** Found with INT or WIS check. DC typically 15.
- **Doors close automatically** behind the party in dungeons (unless spiked open).
