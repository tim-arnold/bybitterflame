# Treasure

## Treasure Value

Treasure found during adventures awards **gold, not XP**. XP comes only from downtime activities between adventures — the app handles this automatically.

## Gold Guidelines by Party Level

Suggested total treasure value per adventure/session:

| Party Level | Gold Value (total) | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| 1 | 20-50 gp | Small coin pouches, cheap gems |
| 2 | 50-150 gp | Modest jewelry, small art objects |
| 3 | 100-300 gp | Significant coin hoards, fine goods |
| 4 | 200-600 gp | Rare gems, gold bars, valuable art |
| 5 | 400-1,000 gp | Major treasure hauls |
| 6+ | 600-2,000+ gp | Legendary treasures, massive hoards |

These are guidelines. Treasure should feel earned and reflect the danger of the adventure.

## Magic Items

### Magic Item Attributes

Magic items are **rare and significant**. They should feel special when found.

#### Properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Bonus** | +1, +2, or +3 to attack/damage or AC |
| **Charges** | Limited uses (e.g., wand with 1d6 charges) |
| **Curse** | Detrimental effect, often hidden until triggered |
| **Sentient** | Has personality, alignment, may conflict with wielder |
| **Attunement** | Requires bonding (typically 1 hour rest with item) |

#### Magic Weapon Bonuses

| Bonus | Rarity | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| +1 | Uncommon | +1 to attack and damage rolls |
| +2 | Rare | +2 to attack and damage rolls |
| +3 | Legendary | +3 to attack and damage rolls |

#### Magic Armor Bonuses

| Bonus | Rarity | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| +1 | Uncommon | +1 AC |
| +2 | Rare | +2 AC |
| +3 | Legendary | +3 AC |

## Treasure Placement

- Treasure should be **guarded** or **hidden**. Never just lying in the open.
- Consider placing treasure in trapped chests, behind secret doors, or on defeated enemies.
- Not every encounter needs treasure. Roughly **1 in 3** encounters should have notable loot.
- The biggest treasure hauls should be tied to the most dangerous challenges.
