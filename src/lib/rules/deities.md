# Deities

All characters choose a patron deity at creation, regardless of class. Priests draw their spells from their deity's power and must perform penance if they critically fail a spellcasting check. Other classes have a patron deity for flavor, oaths, and narrative purposes.

The nine gods are divided into The Four Lords (Lawful and Neutral) and The Dark Trio (Chaotic). Two of the original nine — called The Lost — have had their names expunged from history and cannot be chosen.

## The Four Lords

### Saint Terragnis
- **Alignment:** Lawful
- **Domain:** Righteousness, justice, honor
- A legendary knight who ascended to godhood long ago. She is the embodiment of righteousness and justice and the patron of most lawful humans. Her followers uphold oaths and protect the innocent.

### Madeera the Covenant
- **Alignment:** Lawful
- **Domain:** Law, order, the fabric of reality
- The first manifestation of Law. Every law of reality — a dictate called the Covenant — is written on her skin in precise symbols. She and Memnon are twins, and their eternal struggle defines the cosmic conflict between Law and Chaos.

### Gede
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Domain:** Feasts, mirth, the wilds, nature
- Generally warm and generous, though her wrath calls down violent storms from clear skies. Elves and halflings count among her most devoted followers. Her faithful prize community, celebration, and care for the living world.

### Ord
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Titles:** The Unbending, the Wise, the Secret-Keeper
- **Domain:** Magic, knowledge, secrets, equilibrium
- Ord maintains the balance of arcane power in the world. Shune the Vile schemes endlessly to displace him and seize control of magic for herself. His followers seek hidden truths and guard dangerous knowledge.

## The Dark Trio

### Memnon
- **Alignment:** Chaotic
- **Domain:** Chaos, destruction, primal power, freedom from law
- The first embodiment of Chaos and Madeera's twin. He manifests as a vast, lion-like figure with a crimson mane. His consuming ambition is to tear the laws of reality from his sister and return the cosmos to primordial disorder. His followers see law as a cage and embrace destruction as liberation.

### Ramlaat
- **Alignment:** Chaotic
- **Titles:** The Pillager, the Barbaric, the Horde
- **Domain:** Violence, pillaging, survival of the strongest
- Many orcs and brutal warriors worship Ramlaat. His followers observe the Blood Rite — a creed declaring that when catastrophe comes for the world, only the most powerful will endure. Compassion is a liability; strength is the only currency that matters.

### Shune the Vile
- **Alignment:** Chaotic
- **Domain:** Arcane secrets, sorcery, ambition, forbidden knowledge
- She offers forbidden arcane knowledge to sorcerers and witches desperate or ambitious enough to seek her out. Her long game is to unseat Ord and claim dominion over all magical power. Her followers pursue power without limits and consider any price worth paying.

## The Lost
Two of the original Nine were struck from all records and collective memory. Traces of their existence linger only in obscure manuscripts and places no living person visits. Many folk still swear oaths "Beneath the Eyes of The Nine." The Lost cannot be chosen as patron deities.
