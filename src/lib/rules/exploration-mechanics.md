# Exploration Mechanics

## Delve Turns

Outside of combat, time is tracked in **delve turns** — roughly 10 minutes of in-game time. During a delve turn, the party can:

- Move through a few rooms or down a corridor
- Search a room thoroughly
- Pick a lock or disarm a trap
- Have a brief conversation with an NPC

## Movement

### Climbing

- Climbing requires a **STR check** (or DEX on narrow or fragile surfaces). The GM sets the DC based on the surface — rough stone is easier than slick ice.
- Movement while climbing is **halved**.
- On a failed check, the character slips. If the check fails by **5 or more**, the character **falls**.

### Falling

- A fall deals **1d8 damage for every 15 feet** of distance.
- Maximum 12d8 (180 ft).

### Swimming

- Movement speed is **halved** while swimming.
- A character can **hold their breath** for a number of rounds equal to their **CON modifier** (minimum 1 round).
- After that, **DC 13 CON check** each round or begin drowning (0 HP at start of next turn).
- **Mail:** Disadvantage on swimming checks.
- **Plate:** Cannot swim. Sinks immediately.

## Stealth and Surprise

### Stealth

- A creature attempting to move undetected makes a **DEX check** opposed by the observer's **WIS check**.
- A group uses the **lowest** DEX check among them.

### Surprise

- If one side is completely unaware, the aware side gets a **surprise round** — one full turn each — before initiative is rolled.

### Detecting

- Actively searching for traps, hidden doors, or concealed creatures: **INT or WIS check** (GM sets DC).
- Passive awareness (hearing approaching enemies, noticing something off): **WIS check** at GM discretion.

## Resting

### Full Rest

To benefit from a full rest, a character must sleep for **8 uninterrupted hours** and eat **1 ration** upon waking. A full rest:

- Restores **all HP** and heals **all stat damage**.
- Refreshes abilities marked "once per rest."
- Does **not** reset the Toll. Toll recovery requires specific acts of living (see spellcasting rules).

If the rest is **broken by danger** (combat, alarm, flight), the character must pass a **DC 13 CON check** or gain nothing from the attempt.

### Starvation

A character who cannot eat during a rest does not regain HP or stat damage. After **3 consecutive days** without food, the character takes **1d4 STR damage** each morning until they eat.

## Overland Travel

| Mode | Time per Hex (8 miles) |
|------|----------------------|
| Walking | 5 hours |
| Mounted | 2 hours |
| River or sail | 1 hour |

- A party can travel up to **10 hours per day** before needing to rest.
- Forced march beyond 10 hours: **DC 13 CON check** each additional hour or gain 1 level of exhaustion (disadvantage on all rolls, cumulative).

### Terrain Modifiers

| Terrain | Travel Time Modifier |
|---------|---------------------|
| Road | Normal |
| Plains, grassland | Normal |
| Forest | ×1.5 |
| Hills | ×1.5 |
| Mountains | ×2 |
| Swamp | ×2 |
| Desert | ×1.5 |

## Random Encounters

The GM tracks delve turns and rolls for encounters at the appropriate interval.

### Encounter Frequency

| Area Danger | Check Frequency | Trigger |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| Quiet | Every 4 delve turns | 1 on d6 |
| Tense | Every 2 delve turns | 1 on d6 |
| Perilous | Every delve turn | 1-2 on d6 |

- In total darkness, check **every delve turn** regardless of area danger.

### Determining the Encounter

When an encounter triggers, generate one using the encounter design rules (loaded separately). Choose a category, draw from the environment's creature palette, and apply a reaction roll.

### Area Danger Guidelines

- **Quiet** — travelled roads, settlement outskirts, well-lit corridors
- **Tense** — unmapped areas, dark forests, hostile wilderness, abandoned Underways
- **Perilous** — deep Underways, cursed ruins, active lairs, areas thick with danger

## Reaction Check

When encountering creatures whose disposition is unknown, roll **2d6 + CHA modifier** of the speaking character.

| Roll | Reaction |
|------|----------|
| 2-4 | **Aggressive.** Attacks on sight, issues threats, or bars the way. |
| 5-7 | **Wary.** Suspicious and guarded. Demands to know the party's business. May attack if pressed. |
| 8-9 | **Indifferent.** Neither helpful nor hostile. Willing to talk, unlikely to volunteer anything. |
| 10-11 | **Interested.** Open to conversation, willing to trade or negotiate. |
| 12+ | **Welcoming.** Actively helpful — shares information, offers aid, or invites the party in. |

## Doors

- **Stuck doors:** STR check to force open (DC 13 typical).
- **Locked doors:** Rogue can pick (DEX check, Cunning advantage applies). Otherwise, force open at higher DC or find the key.
- **Secret doors:** Found with INT or WIS check. DC typically 16.
