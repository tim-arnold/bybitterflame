# Spells — Enchanter Path

Unlocked at level 3 upon choosing the Enchanter specialization. Enchanter spells lean toward illusion, charm, mind-reading, misdirection, and bending perception.

---

## Tier 2 (Toll cost: 2 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Veil** | You and up to 3 allies within close range become invisible for 10 minutes. Breaks on any hostile action. Concentration, Sustained. |
| **Puppeteer** | One creature at near range repeats its last action on its next turn, believing it chose to. WIS save negates. |
| **Mirage** | Create an illusory scene up to a near-range area — a bridge over a chasm, a wall across a corridor, a campfire in an empty room. Looks and sounds real. Lasts 10 minutes. Concentration, Sustained. |
| **Thought Snare** | Read the surface thoughts of one creature within near range for 1 minute. They don't know unless you probe deeper (WIS save to detect). Concentration, Brief. |
| **Bewitch** | One creature at near range regards you as a trusted friend for 10 minutes. They'll talk, share information, and let you pass — but won't fight for you. WIS save negates. Ends if harmed. Concentration, Sustained. |
| **Scatter Image** | Create 4 illusory copies of yourself. Each absorbs one attack before vanishing. Concentration, Brief. |

---

## Tier 3 (Toll cost: 3 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Grand Illusion** | Create an illusion up to the size of a building within near range. Sight, sound, smell, and temperature. Lasts 1 hour. Concentration, Lasting. |
| **Dominate** | Take control of one creature at near range for 1 round. They take whatever action you choose. WIS save negates. On a failed save, the creature doesn't realize what happened. |
| **Mindscrub** | One creature at near range forgets the last 10 minutes. WIS save negates. On failure, the gap feels natural — they don't realize anything is missing. |
| **Maze of Mirrors** | One creature at near range is trapped in an illusory labyrinth inside their own mind. Physically incapacitated. INT check each round to escape. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Borrowed Face** | Perfectly assume the appearance, voice, and mannerisms of a specific person you've observed for at least 1 hour. Lasts 1 hour. Even close friends are fooled. Concentration, Lasting. |

---

## Tier 4 (Toll cost: 5 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Living Nightmare** | One creature at near range is trapped in a waking nightmare tailored to their deepest fear. Incapacitated and takes 2d6 psychic damage per round. WIS save each round to escape. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Mass Glamour** | All creatures within near range (up to 10) regard you as a trusted ally for 10 minutes. WIS save negates individually. Those who fail don't realize they've been enchanted. Concentration, Sustained. |
| **Reshape Memory** | Alter up to 1 hour of a creature's memories. You choose what they remember instead. WIS save negates. On failure, the new memories feel completely real. Permanent until dispelled. |
| **Vanishing Act** | You and up to 5 allies become invisible and inaudible for 1 hour. Doesn't break on hostile action — only on taking damage. Concentration, Lasting. |

---

## Tier 5 (Toll cost: 8 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Rewrite** | Permanently alter one creature's personality, loyalties, or beliefs. They believe the change is who they've always been. WIS save negates, and on failure the target gets a new save once per month. Requires 10 minutes of uninterrupted contact. |
| **Waking Dream** | Reshape reality in a near-range area for 1 hour. Walls appear or vanish, gravity shifts, terrain transforms. It's not illusion — it's *real* for the duration. When it ends, everything reverts. Concentration, Lasting. |
| **Erasure** | One creature at near range ceases to exist in the minds of everyone except you. No one remembers them — not allies, not enemies, not family. Their belongings seem ownerless. Lasts 1 day. WIS save negates. If the target dies during the effect, the erasure is permanent. |
