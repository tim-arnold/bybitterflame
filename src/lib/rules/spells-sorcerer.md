# Spells — Sorcerer Path

Unlocked at level 3 upon choosing the Sorcerer specialization. Sorcerer spells lean toward damage, shadow, fear, necromancy, and power drawn from the creeping darkness.

---

## Tier 2 (Toll cost: 2 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Void Blade** | Conjure a blade of solid darkness. Melee weapon, 1d8+INT mod damage, lasts one encounter. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Duskfire** | A burst of cold black flame hits all creatures in a close-range area. 2d6 damage. Doesn't produce light — it consumes it. |
| **Hollow Voice** | Speak a command word. One creature at near range obeys a single one-word command (flee, drop, kneel, stop). WIS save negates. |
| **Nighteyes** | See in total darkness as if dim light, out to far range. Lasts 1 hour. Your eyes turn black. Concentration, Lasting. |
| **Flay** | Touch a creature. 2d4 damage, and they bleed for 1d4 damage at the start of their next 2 turns. No concentration. |
| **Dread Shroud** | Shadows cling to you. All enemies within close range have disadvantage on attacks against you for 1 minute. Concentration, Brief. |

---

## Tier 3 (Toll cost: 3 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Blackfire** | A column of dark flame erupts at near range. All creatures in a close-range area take 3d6 damage. The fire burns cold and leaves no light. |
| **Raise Shade** | Animate the shadow of a recently dead creature (within 1 hour of death). It fights for you for one encounter — same stats as the original but half HP. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Soul Rend** | One creature at near range takes 2d8 damage and can't be healed until the end of their next turn. You feel their pain — take 1d4 damage yourself. |
| **Suffocate** | One creature at near range begins choking. Can't speak or cast spells. 1d6 damage per round. CON save each round to break free. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Cloak of Dread** | Every creature within near range that can see you makes a WIS save or is frightened for 1 minute. Frightened creatures can re-save each round. |

---

## Tier 4 (Toll cost: 5 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Void Bolt** | A beam of pure darkness strikes one creature at far range. 4d8 damage. If this kills the target, their body crumbles to ash. Nothing to raise. |
| **Death Grip** | One creature at near range is paralyzed as shadow tendrils constrict them. 2d6 damage per round. STR save each round to break free. Concentration, Brief. |
| **Desolation** | All living things in a near-range area wither. Creatures take 3d6 damage (CON save for half). Plants die. Water turns brackish. The area remains dead for days. |
| **Dark Pact** | Touch a willing creature. Transfer any amount of your HP to them, or their HP to you. No save if willing. On an unwilling creature, WIS save negates — if it works, drain 3d6 HP from them. |

---

## Tier 5 (Toll cost: 8 each)

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Annihilate** | A sphere of absolute void expands from a point at near range. Everything in a close-range area takes 6d10 damage. No save. Objects are destroyed. The area is left as a perfect sphere of nothing — no floor, no walls, no air. It fills naturally over minutes. |
| **Unmaking** | Touch a creature. Their body begins to dissolve. 4d10 damage per round for 3 rounds. CON save each round to end the effect. If this kills them, nothing remains. |
| **Dark Ascension** | For 1 minute, you become something else. Fly, see in all darkness, immune to nonmagical damage, your spells deal double damage. When it ends, gain 5 Toll. This cannot be reduced by Dark Harvest or any other ability. |
