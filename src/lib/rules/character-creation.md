# Character Creation

## Step 1: Roll Ability Scores

Roll 3d6 **in order** for each ability score: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA.

**Reroll rule:** If no score is 14 or higher, the player may reroll all six scores.

### Ability Score Modifiers

| Score | Modifier |
|-------|----------|
| 1-3   | -4       |
| 4-5   | -3       |
| 6-7   | -2       |
| 8-9   | -1       |
| 10-11 | 0        |
| 12-13 | +1       |
| 14-15 | +2       |
| 16-17 | +3       |
| 18+   | +4       |

## Step 3: Choose Class

### Fighter

- **Weapons:** All weapons
- **Armor:** All armor and shields
- **Hit Die:** 1d8
- **Abilities:**
  - **Grit:** +1 HP per level. Fighters are tougher than everyone else.
  - **Weapon Mastery:** +1 to attack rolls with any weapon.
  - **Gritted Teeth:** Once per rest, recover 1d6 HP as a free action.

### Rogue

- **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, hunting bow, seax, hatchet
- **Armor:** Hide armor
- **Hit Die:** 1d6
- **Abilities:**
  - **Cunning:** Advantage on DEX checks (stealth, lockpicking, traps, acrobatics).
  - **Unseen Strike:** When you attack a creature that doesn't know you're there, deal double damage.
  - **Slippery:** When an attack hits you, use your reaction to halve the damage. Once per encounter.

### Caster

- **Weapons:** Dagger, staff
- **Armor:** None
- **Hit Die:** 1d4
- **Abilities:**
  - **Spellcasting:** Access to cantrips and the neutral spell list. Spells cost Toll to cast (see spellcasting rules).
  - **Arcane Sense:** Feel the presence of magic within near range. Passive, always on — direction and intensity, not details.
  - **Resilient Mind:** Advantage on saves against charm, fear, and mind-altering effects.

## Step 4: Caster Spell Selection (Casters Only)

1. **Cantrips:** Roll or choose **3 cantrips** from the shared cantrip table (20 options). Free to cast, no Toll cost.
2. **Tier 1 Spells:** Choose **4 spells** from the neutral Tier 1 list (12 spells total — 4 Druid-leaning, 4 Sorcerer-leaning, 4 Enchanter-leaning).

The GM notes which spells lean toward which path (Druid, Sorcerer, Enchanter). The player's choices and usage during levels 1-2 inform which specialization the GM offers at level 3.

## Alignment

All characters begin as **Neutral**. Alignment is not chosen during creation — it emerges through play. The GM tracks a character's deeds and may shift alignment to Lawful or Chaotic over time.

- **Lawful:** Upholds order, honor, and the well-being of others.
- **Neutral:** Balance, self-interest, or indifference to moral absolutes.
- **Chaotic:** Freedom, destruction, or service to wild and dangerous forces.

## Step 6: Starting Gear

- Roll **2d6 × 5 gold pieces** for starting gold.
- Characters begin with a **delver's kit** (see equipment).
- Buy weapons, armor, and gear from the equipment tables.

## Step 7: Calculate Armor Class

**AC = 10 + DEX modifier** (unarmored), or use armor AC values.

- Shield adds +2 AC and requires one hand.

## Step 8: Determine Hit Points

Roll your class hit die + CON modifier (minimum 1 HP at level 1).

- **Knockers** add +2 HP at level 1 from Stone Blood.

## Step 9: Final Details

- Choose a name and pronouns.
- Note ancestry abilities, class abilities, and starting gear.
- Record all stats, spells (if Caster), and equipment on character sheet.
