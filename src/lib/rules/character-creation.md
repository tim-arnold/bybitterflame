# Character Creation

## Step 1: Roll Ability Scores

Roll 3d6 **in order** for each ability score: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA.

**Reroll rule:** If no score is 14 or higher, the player may reroll all six scores.

### Ability Score Modifiers

| Score | Modifier |
|-------|----------|
| 1-3   | -4       |
| 4-5   | -3       |
| 6-7   | -2       |
| 8-9   | -1       |
| 10-11 | 0        |
| 12-13 | +1       |
| 14-15 | +2       |
| 16-17 | +3       |
| 18+   | +4       |

## Step 2: Choose Ancestry

| Ancestry | Ability | Languages |
|----------|---------|-----------|

## Step 3: Choose Class

### Fighter

- **Weapons:** All weapons
- **Armor:** All armor and shields
- **Hit Dice:** 1d8
- **Abilities:**
  - **Weapon Mastery:** +1 to attack and damage with one weapon type (choose at creation). Gain additional mastery at levels 5 and 9.
  - **Hauler:** Gain +2 gear slots.
  - **Grit:** Choose STR or DEX at character creation. You have advantage on checks with that stat.

### Priest

- **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, mace, longsword, staff, warhammer
- **Armor:** All armor and shields
- **Hit Dice:** 1d6
- **Abilities:**
  - **Turn Undead:** Make a spellcasting check (DC 10 + undead level). On success, 1d4+priest level nearest undead within near range flee for 1d4+1 rounds.
  - **Deity:** Choose a deity. Grants specific alignment, weapon, and spell access.
  - **Spellcasting:** WIS-based. Learn and cast priest spells from your deity's list.

### Thief

- **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, shortbow, shortsword
- **Armor:** Leather armor
- **Hit Dice:** 1d4
- **Abilities:**
  - **Backstab:** If you attack a creature from behind or that is unaware of you, your attack deals an extra weapon die of damage. This increases by +1 weapon die at levels 5 and 9.
  - **Thievery:** You have advantage on DEX checks to pick pockets, pick locks, disarm traps, move silently, climb, and hide in shadows.

### Wizard

- **Weapons:** Dagger, staff
- **Armor:** None
- **Hit Dice:** 1d4
- **Abilities:**
  - **Spellcasting:** INT-based. Cast wizard spells.
  - **Learning Spells:** You can learn spells from scrolls. Requires a DC 10 + spell tier INT check. Success = spell added to your spellbook. Failure = scroll is destroyed. You can also learn from another wizard's spellbook with their permission.

## Step 5: Choose Alignment

- **Lawful:** Upholds order, honor, and the greater good.
- **Neutral:** Balance, self-interest, or apathy toward cosmic conflict.
- **Chaotic:** Freedom, destruction, or service to dark powers.

## Step 7: Starting Gear

- Roll **2d6 x 5 gold pieces** for starting gold.
- Characters begin with a **crawling kit** (see equipment).
- Buy weapons, armor, and gear from the equipment tables.

## Step 8: Calculate Armor Class

**AC = 10 + DEX modifier** (unarmored), or use armor AC values.

- Shield adds +2 AC and requires one hand.

## Step 9: Determine Hit Points

Roll your class hit die + CON modifier (minimum 1 HP at level 1).

## Step 10: Final Details

- Choose a name (see random tables).
- Note your deity (if priest).
- Record all stats, gear, spells, and abilities on character sheet.
