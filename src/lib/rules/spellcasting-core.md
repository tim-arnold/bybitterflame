# Spellcasting

## Casting a Spell

- Costs your **action** for the turn.
- **Wizard:** Roll d20 + INT modifier vs DC (10 + spell tier).
- **Priest:** Roll d20 + WIS modifier vs DC (10 + spell tier).

### Casting Results

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| Meet or beat DC | **Success.** Spell takes effect. You retain the spell and can cast it again. |
| Below DC | **Failure.** Spell does not take effect. You **lose** the spell until you complete a rest. |
| Natural 20 | **Critical success.** Spell takes effect with **double** any numerical values (damage, duration, range, targets). You retain the spell. |
| Natural 1 | **Critical failure.** Spell fails. See critical failure effects below. |

### Critical Failure

- **Wizard:** You lose the spell until rest AND roll on the **Wizard Mishap Table**.
- **Priest:** Your deity **revokes** the spell permanently until you perform **penance**.

## Focus Spells

- A spellcaster can maintain **one** focus spell at a time.
- Focus spells have ongoing effects that last as long as you concentrate.
- If you take damage while focusing, make a **DC 10 + damage taken** spellcasting check or lose focus.
- Casting another focus spell ends the current one.
- You can voluntarily end a focus spell at any time (no action required).

## Scrolls

- Any spellcaster can cast a spell from a scroll.
- The scroll is consumed on use (whether the casting succeeds or fails).
- Still requires a spellcasting check (d20 + relevant modifier vs DC).
- Non-casters cannot use spell scrolls.

## Wands

- Wands contain a specific spell and have **1d6 charges** when found.
- A spellcaster can use a wand to cast its spell.
- Each use expends one charge.
- Still requires a spellcasting check.
- When the last charge is used, the wand is destroyed.

## Penance (Priest Critical Failure)

When a priest critically fails a spellcasting check, the deity revokes that spell. To regain it:

| Spell Tier | Penance Required |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Donate 10 gp to the deity's temple or spend 1 day in prayer |
| 2 | Donate 50 gp or complete a minor quest for the temple |
| 3 | Donate 200 gp or complete a significant quest |
| 4 | Donate 500 gp or complete a major quest |
| 5 | Donate 1,000 gp or complete an epic quest for the deity |

## Wizard Mishap Tables

### Tier 1-2 Mishap (d6)

| d6 | Mishap |
|----|--------|
| 1 | You take 1d6 damage from arcane backlash. |
| 2 | You are blinded for 1d4 rounds. |
| 3 | You are knocked prone and stunned for 1 round. |
| 4 | A random nearby ally takes 1d6 damage. |
| 5 | A small explosion: all creatures within close range take 1d4 damage. |
| 6 | You are silenced for 1d4 rounds (cannot cast spells). |

### Tier 3-4 Mishap (d6)

| d6 | Mishap |
|----|--------|
| 1 | You take 2d6 damage from arcane backlash. |
| 2 | You are teleported to a random spot within far range. |
| 3 | You are polymorphed into a harmless animal for 1d4 rounds. |
| 4 | All allies within near range take 1d6 damage. |
| 5 | You age 1d10 years. |
| 6 | A hostile summoned creature (level = spell tier) appears within near range. |

### Tier 5 Mishap (d6)

| d6 | Mishap |
|----|--------|
| 1 | You take 4d6 damage from arcane backlash. |
| 2 | You are banished to another plane for 1d4 rounds. |
| 3 | All spells you currently know are lost until you complete a rest. |
| 4 | A rift opens: all creatures within near range take 3d6 damage. |
| 5 | You permanently lose 1 point from a random ability score. |
| 6 | A powerful hostile entity (level 7+) is summoned and attacks everyone. |
