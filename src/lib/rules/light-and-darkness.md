# Light and Darkness

## Distance Bands

All distances use three abstract bands — no grids, no precise measurement.

| Band | Roughly | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| Close | ~5 feet | Sword reach, whispering distance |
| Near | ~30 feet | Across a large room, a stone's throw |
| Far | ~60 feet | Across a cavern, down a long corridor |

## Light Sources

| Source | Radius | Duration | Notes |
|--------|--------|----------|-------|
| Torch | Near | 1 hour (real time) | Held in one hand or set down |
| Lantern | 2× Near | 1 hour per oil flask | Held in one hand, burns oil |
| Light spell | Near | 1 hour (real time) | Cast on an object, hands free |

Torches and lanterns burn in real time — the clock ticks while you play. When a light source dies, you're in the dark unless you light another.

Multiple light sources don't extend duration. They burn simultaneously, and when the last one goes out, darkness falls.

## Darkness

Without a light source, the world turns hostile.

- **Disadvantage on all rolls** that depend on sight — attacks, perception, navigation, reading, picking locks.
- **Random encounters escalate.** In darkness, check for encounters **every delve turn** regardless of area danger level.
- Creatures with natural darkvision can see in darkness to near range as if in dim light.

Darkness in the Underways is not simply the absence of light. It has presence. Things move differently in it. Sound carries wrong. The deeper you go, the more the dark feels like it's watching.
