# Tier 2 Spells

## Tier 2 Priest Spells

**Blindness/Deafness**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One creature you can see must make a CON check vs your spellcasting roll or become blinded or deafened (your choice).

**Bless**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- Up to 3 creatures within near range gain +1 to attack rolls and spellcasting checks.

**Calm Emotions**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- Hostile creatures within near range must make a WIS check vs your spellcasting roll or become indifferent, ceasing hostile actions. Broken by damage.

**Hold Person**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One humanoid you can see must make a WIS check vs your spellcasting roll or be paralyzed. They repeat the check at the end of each of their turns.

**Resist Cold/Fire**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One creature you touch gains resistance to cold or fire damage (half damage).

**Silence**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- A near-sized area you designate becomes completely silent. No sound enters or leaves. Spells with verbal components cannot be cast inside.

**Smite**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Priest | Duration: Instant | Range: Close
- Your next melee attack deals an extra 2d8 radiant damage.

## Tier 2 Wizard Spells

**Acid Arrow**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Far
- A bolt of acid strikes one creature for 2d6 acid damage, plus 1d6 acid damage at the start of its next turn.

**Invisibility**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Close
- One creature you touch becomes invisible. Ends if the creature attacks or casts a spell.

**Knock**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Near
- A locked door, chest, or object within near range opens. Magically locked items require a spellcasting check vs the lock's DC.

**Levitate**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- One creature or object (up to 500 lbs) rises or descends up to 20 ft per round. Horizontal movement is not possible except by pushing off surfaces.

**Mirror Image**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Self
- Three illusory duplicates appear. Each time you are attacked, roll d4: on 1, the attack hits you; otherwise it destroys a duplicate.

**Misty Step**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Instant | Range: Self
- Teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within near range.

**Web**
- Tier: 2 | Class: Wizard | Duration: Focus | Range: Near
- A near-radius area fills with sticky webs. Creatures in the area are restrained. A STR check vs your spellcasting roll breaks free. Webs are flammable.
